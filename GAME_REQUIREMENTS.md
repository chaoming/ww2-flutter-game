# WW2 Turn-Based Strategy Game - Requirements Document

## Overview

A Flutter-based turn-based strategy game set in World War 2, featuring hexagonal map gameplay with diverse terrain types and military units.

---

## Core Gameplay

### Turn Structure
- Players take alternating turns
- Each turn consists of phases:
  1. **Movement Phase** - Move units within their movement range
  2. **Combat Phase** - Attack enemy units within range
  3. **Production Phase** - Purchase new units (if controlling production facilities)
  4. **End Turn** - Pass control to the next player

### Victory Conditions
- Capture all enemy headquarters/capital cities
- Eliminate all enemy units
- Achieve objective-based goals (scenario-dependent)

---

## Map System

### Hexagonal Grid
- Hexagonal tiles for more natural movement and combat angles
- 6 directional movement per hex
- Line-of-sight calculations for ranged units

### Terrain Types

| Terrain | Movement Cost | Defense Bonus | Description |
|---------|---------------|---------------|-------------|
| **Plains** | 1 | +0% | Open grassland, easy movement |
| **Forest** | 2 | +25% | Wooded areas, provides cover |
| **Hills** | 2 | +30% | Elevated terrain, good defensive position |
| **Mountains** | 3 (infantry only) | +40% | Impassable to vehicles |
| **City** | 1 | +35% | Urban areas, can be captured |
| **River** | 2 | -10% | Water crossing, defensive penalty |
| **Road** | 0.5 | +0% | Faster movement along roads |
| **Beach** | 1 | -20% | Coastal landing zones |
| **Sea** | N/A | N/A | Water tiles (naval units only, future expansion) |

### Terrain Effects
- **Fog of War** - Limited visibility based on unit sight range
- **Supply Lines** - Units must maintain connection to supply sources
- **Weather Effects** (optional) - Rain, snow affecting movement and combat

## Units

### Ground Units

#### Infantry
- **Cost:** 100
- **Movement:** 3 hexes
- **Attack:** 5
- **Defense:** 4
- **Range:** 1 (adjacent)
- **Sight:** 2 hexes
- **Special:** Can capture cities, traverse all ground terrain

#### Tank
- **Cost:** 300
- **Movement:** 5 hexes
- **Attack:** 8
- **Defense:** 6
- **Range:** 1 (adjacent)
- **Sight:** 2 hexes
- **Special:** Cannot enter mountains or forests, bonus vs infantry in open terrain

#### Artillery
- **Cost:** 250
- **Movement:** 2 hexes
- **Attack:** 7
- **Defense:** 2
- **Range:** 2-4 hexes (indirect fire)
- **Sight:** 3 hexes
- **Special:** Cannot move and attack same turn, requires spotting for max range

### Air Units

#### Fighter
- **Cost:** 350
- **Movement:** 8 hexes
- **Attack:** 6 (vs air), 3 (vs ground)
- **Defense:** 4
- **Range:** 1 hex
- **Sight:** 4 hexes
- **Special:** Air superiority, intercepts enemy bombers, must return to airfield

#### Bomber
- **Cost:** 400
- **Movement:** 6 hexes
- **Attack:** 9 (vs ground/cities)
- **Defense:** 2
- **Range:** 1 hex (bombing)
- **Sight:** 3 hexes
- **Special:** Strategic bombing of cities/production, vulnerable to fighters

### Unit Mechanics
- **Health Points (HP):** All units have 10 HP
- **Experience:** Units gain experience through combat, improving stats
- **Veterancy Levels:** Rookie → Regular → Veteran → Elite
- **Fuel/Ammo:** Units consume resources during movement and combat
- **Stacking:** One ground unit and one air unit per hex

## Combat System

### Combat Resolution
- Attacker and defender simultaneously deal damage
- Damage = (Attack × HP/10) × Terrain Modifier × Random(0.8-1.2)
- Unit type advantages/disadvantages apply multipliers

### Combat Modifiers
| Attacker vs Defender | Modifier |
|---------------------|----------|
| Tank vs Infantry (plains) | +50% |
| Infantry vs Tank | -30% |
| Fighter vs Bomber | +75% |
| Fighter vs Ground | -40% |
| Artillery vs any | No counter-attack |

### Zone of Control
- Enemy units exert zone of control on adjacent hexes
- Moving through ZoC costs extra movement points
- Cannot move directly from one ZoC hex to another

---

## Production & Economy

### Resources
- **Production Points (PP):** Generated by cities each turn
- Cities generate 5-20 PP based on size

### Production Facilities
- **Cities:** Can produce infantry
- **Factories:** Can produce tanks and artillery
- **Airfields:** Can produce and base air units

### Capture Mechanics
- Infantry units can capture neutral or enemy cities
- Capture progress: 20 points per turn (city has 100 capture points)
- Capturing player gains control once complete
